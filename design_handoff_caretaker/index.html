Caretaker

Video calls for people who forget faces. As each family member speaks, the resident's iPad says who they are and how they're related. Two front ends, deliberately unalike: a resident iPad app for ten-foot reading and one tap, and a family web app for review and control. This file is the single reference for implementing the design in a real codebase.

Read this first. Everything in designs/ is a design reference written in HTML — prototypes that show intended look, copy, and behavior. They are not production code to lift. The job is to recreate these screens inside the target codebase , using its existing framework, component library, routing, and state conventions. If no app environment exists yet, choose the appropriate stack (React + TypeScript is a safe default for the family web app; SwiftUI or React Native for the resident tablet app) and build the designs there.

The prototype files use a small in-house template runtime ( support.js , <x-dc> , <sc-for> , style-hover ). Ignore that runtime entirely — it is a preview harness. Read the markup for structure, the inline styles for exact values, and the logic class for data shape and copy.

Fidelity

High fidelity. Colors, type sizes, spacing, radii, and copy are final — recreate them exactly. Every screen frame in the prototypes is drawn at a fixed canvas size ( 1440×900 family, 1180×820 resident, 390×844 phone, 708×420/440 for isolated empty states) sitting on a #EFEDE6 desk background; those frames are presentation devices, not app chrome. Two areas need judgement rather than copying: photography (all photos are hatched placeholders) and family-tree canvas geometry (hand-positioned in the mock with absolutely-placed 2px connector lines; a real layout algorithm is expected).

Component rules

Buttons — family

  • Primary — #1E5B3C fill, white text, 14–16px/600, min-height: 44px , padding 0 18–28px , radius 12px, no border. Hover #14422B .
  • Secondary (outline) — white fill, 1px #1E5B3C border, primary text. Hover fill #E3EDE2 .
  • Quiet — white fill, 1px #E3EDE2 border, #44544A text. Hover either #FAF8F2 fill or #1E5B3C border.
  • Destructive — only inside a confirmation modal, as a #6A4300 filled button. Never red, never on the page itself.
  • Every family control is at least 44px tall, including chips and filter pills.

Buttons — resident

min-height 72px (header/back), 88px (secondary), 96px (page-level primary), 132px (Answer). Radius 16px, label 28–48px/700, always a word — never an icon alone. Outline variant is white fill with a 3px #1E5B3C border; primary is a flat #1E5B3C fill.

Cards & rows

Family: white surface, 1px #E3EDE2 , radius 12px, padding 14–22px. Resident: white, 3px #E3EDE2 border, radius 16px, padding 20–28px. Selected/own-voice state: #E3EDE2 fill with a 2px #1E5B3C border. Attention: #FBF1DC fill, 1px #EADFC4 . Unresolved/unknown: white fill with a 1px dashed #44544A border (the system's single "we don't know" signal, used for people, voices, and deceased relatives alike).

Chips & status

Pill, radius 999px. Kind labels: 11px mono uppercase, padding 5px 10px. Counts and tags: 12px/600, padding 3–4px 9–10px. Neutral = #F0F0EA / #44544A ; positive = #E3EDE2 / #1E5B3C ; attention = #FBF1DC / #6A4300 .

Avatars

Circle, tint fill with hatched photo placeholder, 600-weight initial in #1E5B3C . Family sizes: 26–32 (rows, waiting-room chips), 36–48 (transcript, modals), 56 (inspector), 72 (person detail). Resident sizes: 40 (tree node), 56 (tree/resident node), 72 (strip), 80–96 (in-call cards), 140–200 (incoming call). Unknown speakers: #F0F0EA fill, dashed #44544A ring, ? glyph in #44544A . The signed-in user's avatar carries the double ring.

Nav

  • Family rail — 232px fixed, white, 1px #E3EDE2 right border, padding 20px 16px , 6px gaps. Brand row: 34px icon + "Caretaker" 16px/700, padding 4px 8px 18px . Items are 40px min-height rows, 14px, radius 10px, padding 0 12px ; active = #E3EDE2 fill + #1E5B3C text + 600; idle = #44544A , hover #FAF8F2 . Order: Dashboard · In call · Calls · Approvals · Family tree · Memories · Med notes · News (Approvals carries a warn-bg count badge). Footer, pinned bottom above a 1px rule: signed-in user (32px avatar, "Ken Chen", "voice enrolled").
  • Family header — 64px, white, 1px bottom border, padding 0 24px : screen title 20px/600, a 13px muted subtitle, then right-aligned actions. (Isolated empty-state frames use a 56px header with a 16px title.)
  • Resident — no chrome and no tab bar. Every non-home screen carries a 72px Home outline button at the top right, in the same place every time; drill-in screens add a Back to… button top left.

(2) A speaker strip : one equal-width card per participant, filling the remaining height — 80px avatar (96px when active), name 32px/700 (36px active), relationship 28px muted. Five documented states (the prototype exposes them as a pill switcher):

  • Nobody talking — all cards at rest weight (3px #E3EDE2 border), no name implied; the bubble holds the last thing said.
  • Active speaker — that card's avatar and name grow, border becomes 3px #1E5B3C , the ct-ring accent pulse plays on the card, and a tint pill reads "Talking now" with a 12px dot. Never color alone, and it is the only animation on screen.
  • Unknown voice — a fourth card appears: dashed #44544A border, #F0F0EA avatar with ? , name "Someone new", relationship "a voice we don't know". No guessed name, no photo; the bubble is attributed to "A voice we don't know".
  • Conversation expanded — a sheet rises over the lower screen (556px tall, #FAF8F2 , 3px tint top border, radius 24px 24px 0 0, the 0 -24px 48px shadow): "Your conversation" 40px/700, a Close button, and bottom-anchored message rows (72px avatar + white bubble, "who" 28px muted over 32px text, max-width 900px).

Responsive behavior

designs/Caretaker Responsive.dc.html · designs/CaretakerScreen.dc.html

F1, F3, and F5 are also built as a single self-measuring component ( CaretakerScreen , props screen = dashboard | call | queue ) rendered at 1440×900 and 390×844 from one implementation. It measures its own container rather than the viewport and crosses over at 720px :

  • Wide (≥720px): the 232px rail, a header action button, and regions that grow ( flex: 1 1 0% ) to fill the height. Controls are 44px.
  • Narrow (<720px): the rail is replaced by a bottom tab bar (white, 1px top border, padding 8px 8px 20px , 4px gaps, tabs flex: 1 at min-height: 52px , labels Home / Call / Approvals / More with badges); the brand mark moves into the header; content becomes one stacked column with flex: 0 0 auto cards and align-items: start ; in-call controls stretch ( flex: 1 1 124px ) to 48px; and Approve goes full-width at 52px / 16px so the queue clears one-handed.
  • Header subtitles shorten at narrow ("Pine Ridge, room 12 · iPad online" → "iPad online"; "5 people · 31 minutes · recording off" → "5 people · 31 min"; "3 waiting · oldest from Sunday" → "3 waiting").
  • Resident layouts are fixed to the iPad and do not reflow. Family layouts are otherwise fluid — minmax(0,1fr) tracks, no fixed widths on text containers.

State & data

Core entities

  • Resident — name, photo, facility + room, device pairing state and last-seen, timezone.
  • Person — id, name, photo, voice status ( not enrolled | enrolled | needs re-enrolment ), speaker color (stable by id), role, deceased flag + years.
  • Relationship — { fromId, type, toId } . Structured types only; all displayed labels ("granddaughter", "great-granddaughter") are derived by traversal , never stored strings, and never typed by a user.
  • Call — participants (including unidentified voices), start/end, floor segments [{ speakerId, startMs, endMs }] (the single source for every timeline), transcript lines [{ speakerId, text, atMs, confidence }] .
  • Correction — a proposal : { callId, atMs, fromSpeakerId, toSpeakerId, proposedBy, status } . Applies only on confirmation by a different family member.
  • Extraction — candidate Memory , MedNote , Person , or Relationship edit, each with the sourcing quote, speaker, timecode, confidence, and status ( pending | approved | rejected ).
  • Memory — approved content attached to a person; only approved items render on the iPad.
  • MedNote — family-only, requires two approvals, hard-walled from resident queries.
  • NewsSettings — master on/off, per-feed toggles, gentle filter, plus a log of held-back items.
  • Consent — per-person, self-granted only; audio retention off by default.

Transitions worth implementing carefully

  • Live attribution: nobody talking → named speaker → unknown voice . Unknown is a first-class state with its own dashed visual language and no guessed name.
  • Correction: proposed → confirmed | rejected . Only confirmed rewrites segments, recolors timelines, and feeds voice-profile training.
  • Approval: pending → approved is the only path to resident visibility; med notes need a second approver.
  • Enrollment: consent → prompts (with redo) → quality check (may demand another prompt) → verification test. Quality is always reported in words, not percentages.
  • Resident reassignment: changing who the resident is rebuilds every derived relationship label — warn before committing.

Accessibility contract

  • Resident side: 28px type floor, 72px minimum control height (88–132px for primaries), 3px borders so edges survive low contrast sensitivity, no color-only meaning ("Talking now" always accompanies the green), no timeouts, no gestures beyond tap and vertical scroll.
  • Family side: 4.5:1 for text (3:1 permitted at 24px+/700), 44px minimum control height including chips, full keyboard paths through the approval queue, visible focus rings, and aria-live="polite" on the live transcript and approval counts.
  • Motion: three loops exist — ct-ring (incoming call / active speaker), ct-dot (typing-style speaking indicator), ct-level (mic meter). Honor prefers-reduced-motion by replacing each with its static end state.
