How this works
Every frame below mounts the same CaretakerScreen component. It measures its own container and crosses over at 720px: above that, the 232px sidebar and the header action button; below, a bottom tab bar and a stacked single column. Approve buttons go full-width at 52px on narrow so the queue clears one-handed. Nothing is duplicated — change the component and both columns change.
